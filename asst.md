Instructions

Each student will create an individual prototype for your group project.  
The purpose is twofold: (1) to give you additional hands-on experience experimenting with AI tools and building a prototype on your own, and (2) to help your group compare and decide which prototype to further develop together.
Requirements
    •    Experiment with AI tools. You are encouraged to use common AI-assisted tools such as Lovable, Cursor, Replit, Bolt, V0, LLMs or others. If you prefer to code directly without an AI tool, that is also perfectly acceptable.
    •    Submit the following:
    ◦    Prototype – Provide a link to your prototype, or a short video/demo showing how it works, or another clear format (please explain).
    ◦    Process documentation – Share the prompts you used, any code you developed (snippets or files), and a brief summary of how you iterated to reach the final version.
    •    The set of individual submissions will allow your group to compare approaches and converge on one prototype to carry forward. In the following week (Week 6), each group will present their chosen "best" prototype and seek feedback from the class via our Zoom breakouts.
